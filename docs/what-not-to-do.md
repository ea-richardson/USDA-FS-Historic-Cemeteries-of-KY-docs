# What Not to Do
Avoid these common mistakes:

## Do not use modern imagery to erase historical evidence.
A cemetery that is difficult to see today may still be correctly represented on the historical map.

## Do not assume a cemetery must still exist.
The current inventory is based on historical map evidence. Field verification is a later phase of this project.

## Do not discard alternate names.
Preserve them in the designated fields or comments.

## Do not invent information to fill a field.
If information cannot be determined reliably:
* leave the field blank when appropriate;
* use the project's designated default where one exists;
* document uncertainty in `Comments`;
* flag for review when necessary.

## Do not silently resolve conflicts.
Document uncertainty and flag unusual cases for review.
